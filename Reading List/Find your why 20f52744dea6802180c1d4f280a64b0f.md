# Find your why

Author: Simon sinek
Finished: No
Image: Find%20your%20why/Screenshot_20250611_003934_Libby.jpg
Notes: Fulfillment is a right, not a lottery. You should feel inspired at the end of every day. If your organization does not do that for you, then you are responsible for making it happen. Inspire people around you. Fulfillment ≠ happiness. Happiness is fleeting. Fulfillment comes when our work connects directly with our why. Everyone has a why. Happiness comes from what we do. Fulfillment comes from why. Lead woth why, not what or how. Why is more emotionally potent. We are not entirely rational beings. How we feel about something or someone is more powerful than what we think. Feelings are difficult to communicate in words. Golden circle: in a target, what = outer ring, how = middle, why = middle bullseye. Every company, team, and individual has a why. Why is a tool, making the abstract tangible. Discovering your why injects passion into your work. How can you find people who believe who you believe if you don't know your own why, your own beliefs? The why of an org: the founder’s story, or elements of the existing culture. The why is an origin story. Bring out your defining moments, gold in your past, etc. The more specific the memories, the better. The quality of the memory matters. At least 10 memories. Identify fhemes.
