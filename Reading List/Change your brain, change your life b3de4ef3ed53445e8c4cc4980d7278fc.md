The capacity to generate goals and achieve them is an evolved function of the pfc, not a socially constructed artifact.  The pfc helps you feel or recognize feelings. Thoughtfulness and impulse control are the pfc.  Underachieving pfc cannot appropriately inhibit the limbic system. Pfc issues increase chances of short attention span, distractability, impulse control issues, hyperactivity, lateness, disorganization, procrastination, unavailability of emotions, lack of perseverance, poor time management, mis perceptions, poor judgment, trouble learning from experience, social anxiety, short-term memory problems.  People with pfc issues often regret things they do, have problems with attention span, and have problems expressing themselves. Social anxiety may be a hallmark of pfc problems.  They have trouble activitating this part of the brain under stress. Social situations require concentration and dealing with uncertainty. Pfc deactivation often causes a person to go blank in conversation causing discomfort.   People with add need extra excitement to kick in their pfc function. The pfc sends inhibitory signals to other parts of the brain. When underactive, it fails to help control other parts of the brain.  Many people with add unconsciously seek conflict to stimulate their pfc. They don’t plan to, and deny it, but they do it. The heightened conflict helps them feel more tuned in.  Worrying increases stress chemicals that keep the brain active. Reactions from others keep them stimulated, or simply being upset increases stimulation. People with add may grind people to pick on, or become the class clowns. Many people with add have “filing cabinets” that only they understand, they are disorganized, chronically late, etc. Many people with add are moody or negative. if they can’t find turmoil with others, they seek it within themselves.   Schizophrenia: distortion of reality from fiction.   Because of poor pfc  activity, they are unable to process their issues, pain, etc., and can develop delusions to explain it. Psychotic disorders often have decreased pfc activity.  Pfc is especially susceptible to injury from head injury, even when there is no loss of consciousness, etc. Your brain is very soft, the skull is very sharp. Minor blows to head can alter character.
