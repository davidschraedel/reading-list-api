Notes: C’s of self: curiosity, calm, confidence, compassion, creativity, clarity, courage, and connectedness. Self-leadership. “Self” is in everyone, like the soul, chi, etc. It is the energy of divinity. The ego is a part of cluster of parts. Self is our seat of consciousness. When you are in self, you don’t imagine yourself from the outside. The Buddhist “no self” is the absence of parts, or the true self and inner being. At our core, all of us are this benevolent being, we can identify with it and be happy to know of our goodness. When we see people as one mind, not multiplicity of parts, then it’s easy to shame and judge them, and label them. When we see the multiplicity in everyone, we can have compassion for them and see that parts of them behave destructively, but that is not who they are. Even those who don’t get good parenting or have good role models have this self to access if they work at it. When you are self-lead, you are present, fully embodied, able to care and be with your parts and other people. When your leg aches, you automatically move your arm to soothe it. This is how our parts operate, and how we behave toward others when in self. When self-lead, you feel both the vulnerability of your parts and to your transcendent aspects. Flow state is self. Self-lead people act morally out of an innate sense of connection to other people and their pain. Shamanism has a similar idea of the inner world, a place to go through song, dance, substance, etc. Internal systems, a family systems model within. Restore self-leadership. When a part agrees to not overwhelm you, it will keep to that agreement. There is nothing inside of you that has power over self when you are in self. Self is more powerful. Parts will begin to open up and relax as they realize they are powerless against self. Respectfully ask parts to relax and listen to them a bit if needed. It’s easy to objective people, and it’s easy to objective parts. If you’re in self, parts that come up are not intimidating to you, they are things you can handle. Why we disembody: protectors mistakenly feel they need to protect the self by bringing it out of body, protectors tend to lead you to acgivities that further disembodyand have disregard for the body, being in your body gives you more access to exiles which scares your protectors, protectors generally think you are the age you were when they needed to step up and you were too weak or unskillful  to protect yourself, when you are mebodied it is more difficult for your protectors to do what they feel is necessary and dominate. Body as a sacred temple that holds the soup or spirit. Self heals. Self needs access to the body.  The goal is to show protectors and other parts that you are much more capable, that they don’t need to do what they have done. When you are not in the drivers seat, parts will likely run the machine. They have the ability to numb your body, create habits that cause harm or friction, etc. Many medical symptoms are exacerbated by parts, while most are not caused by them. Your parts speak to you in the way they can, like pain from arthritis. Our medical system unfortunately often kills the messenger, mutes the symptoms rather than listening. We should not strive to be always in self, Buddha led, etc. We are shooting for a kind family approach, the ability to manage your parts, a soft gentle inner parenting style. Be kind and step in when necessary. Drive the bus, but don’t over-manage or legal your way to discipline. No shame, only compassion and strength. Curiosity. Patience, understanding. Open to the messenger, don’t kill it or cut it off. When a water buffalo is stuck in the marsh, it’s the frogs who suffer. We often keep speaking from the water buffalo, and ignore the frogs who suffer. Speaking protector toward each other instead of compassion toward the exiles or other parts. Care for your parts in a nurturing way. Self energy is contagious. A critical mass creates momentum. Self isn’t attached to immediate success. Self has a long view, a longterm goal of flourishing. May the self be with you (lol).
