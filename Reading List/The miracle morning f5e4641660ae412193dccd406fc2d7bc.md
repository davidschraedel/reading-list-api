Notes: Anitsnooze morning: 1) set an empowering intention before bed, 2) move alarm clock across your room, 3) brush your teeth, 4) rehydrate with water, 5) get into workout clothes. SAVERS. S: silence (prayer, meditation (ifs, mckonkie, embodying helpful emotional states for the day), mindfulness, contemplation), get out of bed and perhaps leave your bedroom. A: affirmations (no lies, active language about what you can do—no passive language), what you are committed to, why it’s important to you, which actions you’ll take and when. Read once a day, recite with emotion. V: visualization (intentional mental rehearsing, imagination, fulfilling certain desires/actions), 1) put yourself in a good mindset, 2) visualize ideal outcome, 3) visualize exactly what you need to do today to progress toward goal. E: exercise. R: read (a good book, self-development, positivity, enoblement). S: scribing (journal your accomplishments, gratitude, learning, progress, goals, joy). You can choose to see the positive, or choose to see the negative. Two pages, two options. Choose gratitude. Some people justify their negativity by saying it’s “realistic”. But what’s realistic is that you have a lot to be grateful for, and you can choose to be grateful, or choose to be in turmoil. Celebrate the good. What we focus on determines how we feel. ABC: accept life exactly as it is, be grateful for each moment, choose your optimal state of consciousness (peace). All inner turmoil is caused by our resisting reality. We naturally wish things were different, and are unaware that this resistance to reality is causing our emotional pain. Things out of our control are not worth pain. Resisting doesn’t change those things, just our increase in pain. Choose acceptance, not resistance. Eating makes you tired, so eat after the miracle morning. Drink water when you wake up to rehydrate.  30 days to form a habit: first q0 days are unbearable, second 10 days are uncomfortable, and third 10 days are unstoppable. After this, you are well-equipped to keep this habit going. In the first ten days, people don’t realize that these first days are temporary, that the unbearableness will go away after you get past a certain number of days. It will not always feel that terrible, it will get easier. Any moment can be the best of our life if we are truly present and grateful. We choose how we interpret life experiences. Living the miracle morning, evening, life, is not just for you, but for others as you bring your best self to them.
