Notes: Everyone has peaks and valleys in their life. Peaks and valleys are connected: the errors today create tomorrow’s good times. Good times often lend tomorrow’s errors. We create our own good and bad times. Peaks = times when you appreciate what you have. Valleys = times when you long for what is missing. We create valleys and peaks in our own mind. When we enjoy what is good, we create peaks. We can’t control many things, but we can control our mind. (Gratitude). Choosing a better belief gives you a better result. What we believe makes a difference. People like those who create peaks. Find and use the good that is hidden in a bad time. The real voyage is having new eyes. Bad things happen because of what we did in good times. Good times happen because of what we did in bad times. The essence of knowledge is having it to use it. How you manage your valley determines how soon you reach your next peak. If you do not learn in a valley, you become bitter. If you learn something valuable, you become better.  Plateaus are stagnant, like a dead heart beat. They can be a time to rest, but do not offer life and energy. You are not your peaks or valleys. It’s how you feel about peaks and valleys that makes a difference. It is not the good or bad time itself, but how we manage things then, how we feel about it. The source of fear is often the ego. On a peak, ego sees things better than they are. In a valley, ego sees things as worse than they are. Ask yourself, “what is the truth in this situation?”. What can I do right now to help? Our fears are about us, not others. Get outside of yourself, putting your ego aside. When you look at a valley, what do you see? Pain? Can you see the good in it? Create and follow your own sensible vision: a peak that you want to be on, the senses that are real, imagine yourself in your future enough that you soon enjoy doing the things that will take you there. The pain in a valley can wake you up to a truth you have been ignoring. You are always creating a vision of your future, awares or not. Either fearful vision or a sensible vision. Your valley can be fear itself. Move beyond the fear, and you do much better.  Hold onto your sensible vision with all your senses. A personal peak is a triumph over a fear. Stay true to what you want, doing the things that take you there. The vision of the peak dissolves fears, and gives clarity to move on. When you are in a valley, imagine seeing what you might from a peak, alowing yourself the clearer view. Make reality your friend. Face and embrace the truth. Truly look for what is real, rather than live in illusion. The peak is to celebrate (give thanks) life, and the valley to learn of life. A sensible vision is realistic, attainable, and sensable. “Truly”: what is real. When we take peaks for granted, we forget curiosity, and truly seeking. What is the truth of this situation? What is the good hidden in this hard time? Instead of becoming discouraged by your fear, say less and do more, see that hidden good may come from your “loss”. You get out of a valley sooner when you manage to get outside of yourself, by serving others and being more loving.  Notice what puts you in valleys, and do the opposite. A peak is a time when you appreciate what you have. Look at what is real, not what you wish for or fear is real. Make reality your friend. Relax, knowing that valleys end. Be humble and grateful.  Valleys may free you from what you did not want to see or understand. Remember: What is the truth in this situation? What is the hidden good? Be of service to others. Peaks and valleys are connected. The wise things you do in today’s valleys create tomorrow’s peaks. Do more of what got you to a good place. Look outside yourself. Wishing leads to no action. Make reality your friend, accepting what is. Imagine what you where you want to be, and the steps you need to take to get there. You can control what you do. Do the things that will take you to the peak you created/imagined inside. There will be tough times ahead, and you will have many opportunities, to learn and share with others. Life and everything living moves in circles, with highs and lows. A parable leads you to discover for yourself, letting you learn and use it for yourself. Make reality your friend because: things are not as good as they seem on the peak, and they are not as bad as they seem in the valley.  How do you recognize the truth in a situation? Recognize the value of truth, the importance of it. How’s your current approach working? If it’s not working, it probably isn’t true. If it is lowering your energy, stressful, etc., it probably isn’t true. It should Raise your energy and make you feel more peaceful. How do you find the good in the bad time? Good will serve you well, it will be to your advantage, will help you, raise your energy. How do we practice humility and gratitude when times are good? We have our own wisdom within us, and we know the truth. When we learn to trust truth and rely on it, we become more calm, in touch with reality. We realize that life is ups and downs, and we don't attribute every success or failure to ourselves. We realize that we are only part of everything, a part of what is good, of our failures, of the rest of the world. How do we stay true to what we really want? Better put as “what we really need”. We are one degree off all the time. Ask yourself questions to see that you are off course, and course correct. Make small corrections, small choices, realigning ourselves with truth, with reality, accepting what is, stay9ng humble, grateful, keeping a sense of humor. Remember the bad times and your mistakes. How do we share this? Live it. We share by living as role models. People learn by seeing others truly live happier and more aligned with truth.
