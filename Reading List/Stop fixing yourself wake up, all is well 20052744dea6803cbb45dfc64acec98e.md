# Stop fixing yourself: wake up, all is well

Notes: You have everything you need. You can already be happy. We are programmed from birth to believe that we need things to be happy, things in the future, attributes, etc., to be happy. Our beliefs are false. Want to get rid of your suffering? Drop your false beliefs. Love embraces the whole of life. Your attachments to things drain the love of life. We are programmed to not challenge the programming. Love requires objectivity and observation. You are not deliberately causing yourself unhappiness. Do not blame your programming. Let go of attachment to things and letting them give you happiness or displeasure. It is selfish for others to demand we choose them over ourselves. It is not selfish to achieve your own happiness, to let go of attachments, to not seek pleasure from others. If you let go of your attachments and cravings, you cease to drive with your breaks on. When you love for nothing, you have all your skills and energy, you’re relaxed and you don't care. This is being awakened. No need for praise or things to make you happy. The ancients did not care for honor. Gaming or gaining the world means you lose yourself. Only the adult being is able to be one thing and pretend to be another. The way of self-understanding: learn as much as possible, regardless of the outcome. Non-selective, never rigid, transparent, etc. For the things you fear: this too shall pass away. Happiness is not conscious, experienced. The cause of unhappiness is false belief. Our programming is so strong that we don't have a suspicion that we could be wrong, that we should doubt at all. Most people are in a dream, with no idea they are dreaming. True happiness is not gained or caused, it is a natural state. You don’t experience happiness because you need to drop attachments. Through awareness: observe that a belief is false, and it drops. Awareness. Happiness is a grace, called awareness.  What you are aware of, you are no longer a slave to. When you have attachments, you obsess and seek with earnest, but awareness of this will allow you to drop the attachment. Observe yourself, clearly, without judgment, and experience marvelous changes in self. What should you be aware of? Reactions, relationships. Effortlessly a lover going to their partner, or a hungry man eating. The “effort” is fun, joyful, not toil. No power outside of yourself has the power to make you happy or unhappy. No event justifies a negative feeling. You create the negative feelings based on your programming. Any sorrow for your circumstances is an attachment. Don't identify with your negative feelings. Say “gloominess is there, depression is here”. The harder you try to change yourself, the harder it gets. Don't get tense trying to relax. Don't make a goal of it. Come to reality and let the happiness take care of itself.  You can be happy in your anxiety, or in your depression. How can I make it last? - that’s not happiness, that’s addiction. Serenity prayer. Many things you cannot change. In saying yes to what you cannot change, you are saying yes to God. You will have peace. Perspective: this too will pass away. If what you have is love, hold onto the compassion and matter-of-factness of observing the objective of your attachments, then you will love it and love all else just the same. The orchestra will play a music of its own. Nothing can put it there or take it away. Nothing else will matter anymore. See reality through the eyes of a child. The enlightenment meditation: acceptance let’s feelings surface and dissapate. This can cause pain, but also peace. Only love and understanding can lead to lasting change. Lion parable: the lion believed he was a sheep until he saw his reflection and let out a mighty roar. If we really knew who we are, there wouldn’t be any wars. I can observe the “me”. This is what we think about. Is “i” really changing? Know. I am not my body. We can change the labels without changing the I. Your name, beleifs, etc. are not the I. Do not label. When you are caught up in labels, what value do you have? Labels relate to “me”. Suffering exists in “me”. Children are innocent. They are what they are. Only adults pretend to be something they are not. The world trains then to be something else and hide their true self for so long that they forget it exists and no longer know whi they are. The desire to become is part of this. You are already something. There child surrenders to its nature to be what it is, to live. All you can do is silence the monkey mind and let the heart speak. You have prostitutes yourself for the sake of competition and a goal to become. The world: courting applause and success and fame. If you allow yourself to feel the emptiness of this hell on earth, you may experience pain that would awake you from this slumber. Dare to abandon your teacher and see everything without fear or formula, and see for yourself. If you understand who “I” is, you will be free, fear no one, not care about embarrassment, etc. Life is for the risk taker, not the coward. Those who lose their life shall save it. Those who save it shall lose it. Dead people have an inbuilt fear of change. When you serve, you see. To love is to be sensitive to life and things, persons, responding from sensitivity. Love is not blind. Love is not attachment. Love excludes no one at all, but embraces the whole of life. Your cannot come to possess this kind of love, benevolence for all, who you may not even know. It is already present within you. Remove the blocks you place in front of sensitivity. Blocks of belief, attachment, and fear. Labels cease our seeing, they are beliefs that keep us from seeing. Tension and anxiety removes us from love. Being loved does not cause being loving, but instead euphoria, a temporary switch in the brain. Attempt to see those you love or dislike anew, as if you saw them for the first time. Labels are us seeing through our past. Search for treasures buried at our disposal. See others anew, and see yourself anew. Not judging others will allow you to not judge yourself. Truth is a mystery, and love means seeing the ignorance of all beings, including you. Adopt the mind of a little child, responding to reality, rather than labels that have been programmed into them. You will be appalled to find that your response to others is rarely toward them but instead your programming, beliefs, whether positive or negative. You stereotype to walk with ease, but not with love and understanding and clear thinking. Search for your bias. Charity is pleasure masquerading as self-lessness. A saint is one until he knows it. The worst kind of charity is doing something to avoid a bad feeling. Everything you are irritated with someone or something, look within and say “the cause of this irritation is not them, but me”. How are you causing yourself irritation? Could it be what you are irritated reflects something in your own life, or something in you? Are you irritated because they fail to live up to the standards and expectations that have been programmed into you? Given their life, background, and programming, they cannot help but behave as they do. See this and you will respond with love. How foolish of you to threatening they live up to expectations that your parents programmed into you?  Am I in charge of this situation or is the situation in charge of me? You become in charge when you stop letting the situation cause you irritation from your pgoramming, when you stop responding with programming, not subject to the situation. Irritation will only make you less effective. Search for this person’s defects in yourself, and your anger will turn to gratitude for self-awareness. Being evil is a sickness caused by a lack of consciousness. Before you used to be controlled, now you can see others as opportunities, to avoid no one and to go anywhere. Evil is a lack of awareness, a sickness. Awareness and understanding turns bitterness to compassion. Silence: the first revelation it brings is yourself. It will bring you things no money or influence can, such as peace. As the quality deepens, so will your quietness, and then change. Silence will become power to you. Your ideas of God are all inadequate, they fall short. Look, listen, hear, and see. God dances the world. He is in creation. Beauty is a way of seeing. In the heart. Look at the dance, and perhaps you will recognize the dancer. Suffering is when you clash with reality, out of touch with the truth. Suffering points out that there is falsehood somewhere. Identifying ourselves with anything, is a falsehood, and creates suffering. What leads to growth is painful experiences. If we knew how to use suffering, how we would grow. Observe disappointment, what does it say about you? Every feeling is useful for understanding, for growth. Every painful event contains in it a seed of growth. Whatever causes you painful feelings is a teacher. What does that emotion say about you, or way of life, your perception, your programming? You will suddenly find you are full of gratitude for that event or person that brought in you negative emotions. You will find grace for others. Your causing pain in others is a seed of growth for them. All is growth. Dawn meditation: listen to creation greeting the day. What sentiment do you think creation is expressing as it wakes up? There is a song too in your heart. If you have never heard the song, you have not listened. Listen to your heartworm blending with creation all around you. Give up your dependence on your drug. Though you will not be of the world, you will continue to live in it. You will have nowhere to lay your head. Develop a taste for the good things in life: take on activities you can do with your whole being, where you are so engaged that you care not for approval, success, or recognition. Return to nature and commune with creation. Drop the words and concepts and contact reality. You will become more energetic, alive, etc.
