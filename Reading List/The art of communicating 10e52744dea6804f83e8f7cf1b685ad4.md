# The art of communicating

Notes: We need good nourishment. Are we ingesting healthy, loving, communication? Giving out communication is also a form of nourishment, as it affects us and we take it in. Be wary of giving toxic communication. Try to produce more compassion, instead of consuming toxins in the communication around you. We consume with our eyes, ears, etc. In a relationship, we are nourishment for each other, and must be Mindful of the communication we feed each other. Every thought you produce is feeding you and your relationships. We yearn for love, but don’t know how to produce it for ourselves. We look to technologies to fix our loneliness. There is no device that makes up for our failure to communicate well internally. Even if others don’t get our message, writing or communicating kindness is nourishing to us. Put more compassionate communication out into the world. When you look at your phone, remember it is a means to your compassionate communication. Mindful breathing is a means of communication with the mind. When we breath in, we know we are breathing in, etc. Nonthinking: not saying or doing anything. Mindful breathing is the practice of nonthinking. Take your mindfulness with you, more so than your phone. When you sit mindfully, your body and mind are in the same place, which is a rarity. If you think while you walk, you’re not really walking. Mindful steps bring in touch with our bodies, and with the earth. Fear and anger may come up, but we embrace them, rather than telling them to go away. Our suffering includes the suffering of our ancestors.  Consumerism markets offer us things to cover up our suffering, to consume when we don’t need to consume. Suffering brings happiness. We need suffering to grow happiness, because they always go together. We handle happiness like we do suffering. Listen to your suffering. We are coresponsible for the suffering in ourselves and in others. When you understand your suffering, you begin to understand others. Self love is crucial to loving others. Compassion arises when we understand the suffering in others. Coming home to ourselves is the first step, then we can see others. We all profit from your true happiness. Take time each day to communicate with yourself, for mindfulness. The Buddha is simply the most compassionate or loving type of person there could be, so there is a Buddha inside all of us.  Deep listening is listening for the intent of decreasing the suffering in another. If you can listen with compassion, you can help the other person suffer less. We have to practice compassion, so that we can truly listen with compassion, as this is often difficult and they will undoubtedly say things that cause us to react. When you listen deeply, you know that the other person suffers. You don’t want to blame the other person. We don’t have enough understanding and love, and this is what we thirst for. waiting has serious consequences. Love being present mean there is mutual understanding. If it is not, ask “do I understand you enough?” just because you spend a lot of time with people, it doesn'team you understand them. We spend a lot of time with ourselves and don’t understand or listen to ourselves. Loving speech is saying things mindfully in a way that helps otjers recognize the suffering in you and others.  What you say and write should convey only compassion and understanding. Your words can inspire generosity and openness. Right speech is loving speech. When we practice right speech, we and others feel wonderful. We are critical when we don’t understand. Right speech: tell the truth (don’t lie or turn the truth upside-down), don’t exaggerated, be consistent (no double talk manipulation,integrity), use peaceful language. We tell the truth mindfully, trying not to make them suffer needlessly. This takes practice, to speak truth in a way others want to hear. Speak the language of the world or in ways they can understand, in a way that reflects absolute truth, according to the understanding of each individual (or the person) listening, prescribing the right medicine for the disease. Make these truths relevant to the mind of the person listening. We share information in a way people can integrate, according to their situation, like a child learning of death, etc. Like a good journalist, we tell the truth, but in a way that we don't water the seeds of fear, anger, vengeance, etc. When people understand, their anger will lessen, and they know what to do. We want to create fewer terrorists (those with wrong perceptions), not more. Everyday we can say something that has the capacity to heal and help people. The 6 mantras of loving speech: 1: “I am here for you” (i am present for you), 2: “I know you are there, and I am very happy” (your presence is a gift), 3: “I know you suffer, and that is why I am here for you” (mindful awareness of suffering, simply offering your presence), 4: “I suffer. Please help” (I do need you, especially when I suffer), 5: “this is a happy moment” (we are lucky to be here), 6: “you are partly right” (retaining humility, in the face of praise or judgment, accepting what is in the here and now). When you suffer, remember you need others, especially if they are the ones you feel have harmed you. I suffer, and i want you to know it. I am doing my best to practice mindfulness. Please help.these mantras create a home, a safe space for yourselves and others.we must heal our anger, not practice or suppress it. Go home to yourself. Be present, look deeply at your strong emotions. Go home to put out the fire, instead of going after the arsonist. Ask for help when you are angry.those we are closest to are often those we suffer from most. Understanding breeds healing and mutual help. Make the words your own, your own loving kindness and compassion. Loving speech opens the door, then deep listening takes place. The suffering of pride: we live with wrong perceptions every day. Don’t let pride keep you from mindful communication and inquiry. You are the continuation of your ancestors, receiving suffering and habits from your parents. Practicing mindful awareness and Reconciliation can change the cycle. Generate the energy of compassion and forgiveness in yourself. In longterm relationships, we forget that change is possible. We often wait for others to change. Don’t interrupt when someone speaks wrong perceptions. Understand deeply first, then change is possible. In most relationships, not troubled by abuse, separation is not a solution, as you will continue to suffer from the unhealed relationship. When parents divorce, kids or other issues keep them interacting with their spouse. There is no alternative to communication. The question is if you will try to understand each other.  When we listen deeply, we begin to understand that others suffer much the same as we suffer. We remember that they are truly human, as we are. We feel compassion for them. In the beginning, we think we are the only ones with fear and suffering. But we soon find out they have a lot of fear and suffering , fear of us, etc. We suffer less already by seeing their fear for what it is, and nurturing a compassionate thought. There’s no place where deep listening and loving speech are inappropriate. Communication is as much a part of your work as is the end product. Your communication at work starts with your commute. Is your commute mindful? Your communication creates an atmosphere, the thoughts, words, and actions you produce influence you and others. Learn to listen to everyone with equal interest and concern. Set up spaces for mindful breathing, the beginning of mindful communication. Greet people with loving kindness, answer the phone with compassionate communication. Meetings can be better with some mindful breathing. Start the meeting with a statement that participants will be open and respectful towards the thoughts and concerns of others. When we practice alone, our way of communicating becomes a bell of mindfulness toward others. We have influence when we lead by example. The root of communication and community, to share, impart, or make common. Without a connected community, it is difficult to do anything at all to positively impact the environment, freedoms, etc. A hungry ghost = someone who has a very big stomach and appetite, but a very small throat. Some do not believe in love and understanding, and thus cannot receive it. Be patient with these people, and give them space and compassion. With patience and time, they may begin to see the love and understanding within you. Do not be too eager. Community building takes time. Community strengthens compassion, because of altruism. See yourself in community, and see community in you. Our communication is our continuation. Our thoughts, speech, and actions are our fruit we produce. When we communicate, we either produce more compassion and harmony, or more suffering. Our communication is our Karma. We communicate in every moment with ourselves or others. You are what you do. Thinking is nourishment or poison, so it is already action. To produce a thought is to act.  Everything we say and do bares our signature. the cloud continues in the grass, trees, etc. that it watered. In every cell of our body, our ancestors are there.  Right communication today can help us heal the past, enjoy the present, and prepare the ground for  a good future. Talk to your inner child several times a day. Embrace your wounded child. Write a love letter to persons you have difficulty with, practicing loving speech. Whether you send it or not, it changes you. Sign a peace treaty or peace note: 1. Refrain from saying or doing anything that might cause further damage or escalate anger. 2. Not suppress anger. 3. Practice breathing and taking refuge in the island of myself. 4. Calmly, within 24 hrs, tell the one I am angry with about my anger and suffering, verbally or with a peace note. 5. Ask for an appointment later in the week, to discuss this matter more thoroughly. 6. Not say “i am not angry”. 7. Practice breathing and looking deeply into my daily life, to see how i have been unskillful at times, how i have hurt the other person, how the seed of anger within is me is the primary cause of my anger, how the other's suffering watering my anger is the secondary cause, how the other is only seeking relief from their suffering, that as long as the other suffers i cannot be truly happy, apologize immediately as soon as i recognize my unskillfulness and lack of mindfulness, postpone the meeting if i do not feel calm enough to discuss the anger. Make an appointment with others to look at the hurt or suffering one or both of you experience.  Begin anew. Recognize others’ good traits. Water the flowers of kindness and compassion, taking power away from the weeds of anger. Beginning Anew: Flower watering: showing appreciation for wholesome qualities in others. This is not flattery or insincerity. Express regret for anything you have done to hurt others: let go of resentments or regrets. Express feelings of hurt: Listen deeply. When the atmosphere becomes unpleasant or heavy., take a few deep breaths, then say “I just remembered we have a cake in the refrigerator”. Even if you don't have have a cake, it is a message to everyone to remember the good that you have. Make a snack or such and invite people to join you. Silent Hugging: hug deeply for each of the following mindful breaths… being aware that you are here in the present moment and happy, being aware that the other is present in this moment and they are also happy, being aware that you are here right now together on this earth with great gratitude for your togetherness. You can also practice it as: aware that you are both her and alive, thinking of where you will be in 300 years, remembering that you are both here and alive. Hugging can be a great form of Reconciliation., because life becomes real in that moment.
