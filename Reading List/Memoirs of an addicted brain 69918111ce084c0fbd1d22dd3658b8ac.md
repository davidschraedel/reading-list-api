# Memoirs of an addicted brain

Author: Marc lewis
Date Finished: May 4, 2024
Finished: Yes
Image: Memoirs%20of%20an%20addicted%20brain/20240504_110723.jpg
Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Notes: A captivating story, presenting a view of the issues and developmental setbacks that lead to and prolong/encourage addictions, as well as descriptions of the neuroscience/brain changes that occurs in this process. I found it useful to consider the internal dialogue and experiences that the author had, and how that led him to substance abuse and a long-lasting addiction. It all started with not having a safe space when he was young. When he got bullied, he had no home to return to, no loving parents to welcome him when he was not welcome other places.
