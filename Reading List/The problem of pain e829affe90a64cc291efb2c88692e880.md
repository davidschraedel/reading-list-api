Notes: We regard God as a parachute, and pain forces our attention. Happiness is not enough to make us blessed. God stoops to humility in allowing us pain for our good, for us to have humility and choose Good or God. Death into life. Surrender to God. Pain hurts. Suffering is not good in itself, but in it we can direct ourselves to God. God can make good out of evil. You will serve God however you choose, as Judas or John. It makes a difference to you, but not to God, he makes good of whatever happens. Fasting is different from Missing dinner by accident is different or poverty. Self torture is different from tribulation. Only God can mortify. If tribulation is necessary for redemption, then it will not cease til redemption is finished. We seek security to find comfort in this world, God helps us find insecurity in mortality. Repentance requires correcting the error, and the temptation. But pain requires no such undoing. Suffering only produces pity.
