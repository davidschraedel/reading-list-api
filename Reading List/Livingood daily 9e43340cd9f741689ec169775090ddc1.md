Notes: Would you trade your arms and legs for $15 million? Health is your biggest asset. People don’t plan on getting truly sick, but most do. Medicine was created for emergencies. Health care is not designed to make people healthy. How would you fix a pond full of sick fish? Clean up the water. The U.S. has terrible rates of death by disease and injury compared to similar countries. Its medical system is one of the leading causes of death and injury in the nation. We don’t have a “Healthcare system”, we have a sick-care system. Your health is up to you, no one will come for you. It is your responsibility. Programs are short-lived. Habits last, your way of life lasts. Health is like a formula. Health is all about function, not looks or just feelings. Usually, when you are at 60% of functioning and healing (or health) is when  you have your first symptom. So when you have symptoms, your health is way below what it seems. You don’t feel your kidney problems until 10% functioning usually. We can’t judge our health simply by how we feel. We can treat symptoms, or get to the cause. Start building health to get it. You are your greatest doctor. Your body heals itself, and is always running at 100% power. But you can’t get the power if there is interference. The three T’s of interference: Thoughts, toxins, traumas. Give your body good soil to grow in, removing interferences, and it will grow and flourish. Thoughts: gratitude (put things in perspective), take a break (mindful walk, be still, spend time in rest and digest state, get oxygen by breathing, , sleep). Fix your food. We idolize food, making everyday a “vacation” day. Start with 21 days of reset with food: cut sugar, eat more fat, eat clean protein,eat good nutrients, don’t eat too much or snack all the time. Most of our excess weight is sugar, not from fat.  Temporarily take a break from all sugars: flour and grains, sugar water, alcohol, fruit (except grannysmith apples, grapefruit, limes, lemons, and some berries),  tubers (potatoes, sweet potatoes, yams, minimize carrots), sweeteners and fake sweeteners. Use Stevie if needed. Do this for 21 days, just to break your sugar addiction, then add healthy sugar back in, like honey, fruit, whole grains, potatoes, etc. For fat, use olive oil, and cook with coconut, avocado, and grapeseed.  Avoid oil-roasted nuts. Avoid milk and dairy. Eat good fats like nuts and avocados, seeds, etc. Eat clean protein: pasture-raised chickens, moderate pork (well-fed, organic), organic grass-fed cows, wildcaught fish, avoid bottom feeders, eggs, no soy (#1 good food). Avoid corn. Focus on getting nutrients. Fuel the vehicle with nutrient dense snacks, like peppers, cucumbers, broccoli, other veggies, hummus, boiled eggs, avocados, raw nuts, granny Smith apples, coconut oil, greens, supergreen smoothies, etc. . Try fasting. Mult0le methods: smoothi smoothi dinner, or eat within a 6-hour window (intermittent fasting) like 12pm to 6pm, or rotational fasting (switch up the 6-h9ur window each day).
