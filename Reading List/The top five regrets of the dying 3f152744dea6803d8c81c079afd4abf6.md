# The top five regrets of the dying

Author: Bronnie ware
Finished: No

Notes: Love and kindness are gifts, not deserved. Therefore we can be grateful for them. We don’t need to feel unjustly dealt with when others are unkind, because that is to be expected. Respond with compassion. It is a gift to have received love. We must live our life as we see fit. There is no need to worry about losing others’ kindness and love, because it was never ours to hold or control anyway, it was extra beauty and joy in our life. Live a life true to you, not what others expect you to do. You cannot control their affection. Conflict is inevitable in all families and groups. Lack of outward conflict is not the same thing as fulfilling connection and earned peace. Regret 1: I wish I'd had the courage to live a life true to myself, not the one others expected of me. It is a beautiful thing to see others living true to themselves. Be happy for them. Love them for not having to others’ expectations.  We are products of our environment.
