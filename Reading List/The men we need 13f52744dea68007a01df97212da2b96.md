Notes: A real man: women feel safe, a sense of security. The man as protector. It’s how women and babies look at him. Firefighters are attractive because they save people. When we prove we are willing to help, our character shines. Women don’t respect passive men, because they know they are meant for more, because a passive man is not a protector. They like men who show up, who know who they are. Adam was created to tend the garden, to look after it. Masculinity is about taking responsibility, being humble consistent dedicated keepers of the garden. What we need is the willingness to bring whatever resources we have, not superstrength, etc. The Man is like God, he looks after the needy and poor, the sick and afflicted. (Blessings, confidence in doing what you can, showing up). We can’t just do what we want, because others’ lives will be worse off because of our absence. They need good men. God called Adam when he hid, because Adam was the keeper of the garden, charged with responsibility. Actively do unto others, serve, help, defend, keep, care. A passive man is a threat to the woman, because he may be not up to the job of keeping her, of caring for her. Passive men are much more common than aggressive or “toxic” men. If you lived your life without commitment to anyone, what would be the point? How would you feel? Real women are challenging, but worth it, meaningful. Historically, we had no choice but to grow up. In our modern world, we can truly be useless, and never learn to grow up.  Be freaked out by the possibility that you might not become the man you were meant to be. Become that man. Sin is a rush of sweet, followed by bitter pain. A sweet fruit that becomes a pile of gravel.  Don’t fall for the swindle, the shortcut, the counterfeits. People are literally buying robots for connection, when the real thing, other humans, are all around them. The real female mind adds to, challenges, and refines us. Porn and fake sex are fakes. My sin is sin because it stops me becoming who I could be,preventing me from the kingdom of heaven.  Satan fishes with counterfeit exaggerated feed. None want the fake life, but they fall for it. Satan can only destroy or mock, replacing our meaning with meaninglessness.  Dopamine doesn’t give pleasure, it causes us to seek it, to look for pleasure. Dopamine issues are overseeking, raging fires, that are never satisfied. Overzealous longings we keep throwing gas and flammables on.  When you get over your dopamine addiction, you will realize the nonvirtual reality is just the reality you were made for. People around you should be safer because you are there. Are you looking out for others’ safety? Be aware of what’s happening around you (don’t be on your phone all the time with others or in public).  People need challenges. Males need threats, challenges, ways to help people. We need purpose.we are stewards. We are god’s hands, he expects us to start the car, wash dishes, work, garden, etc. He doesn’t do our work for us. What we do matters (it’s a wonderful life). If you need to do something, don’t assume it will get done. You are in your specific situation, others may not do it. You are there to help, do it. Others can’t do what you may. What we do has real consequences. The bystander effect: people do just stand by and watch. The treason is real. Your strength can be a threat to others, but your weakness can also be such. A woman will bet her future on you. Don’t invade your own garden. Our culture trains us to not tend our garden. Choose to be a keeper of your garden, a man among boys. It’s your job to shelter your family in appropriate ways. You are to create safety for the vulnerable. You must know your family well to be able to help and shelter them. You are here to shape character in your kids, not just skills. You can take anything too far. Most men don’t overprotect. Give them challenges, but filter what you give them to their age and temperament. If your kid has asthma, would you pump exhaust into their face? Would you show your child violent material and porn if they have a tendency or weakness toward such, pain with it? What are we creating for the people in our own home? A man creating danger in his own home is life altering, and extremely troublesome. Choose protection, care, mercy, forgiveness, and start the journey now. Set the tone, start planting these seeds. Be a kangaroo, hold the baby, give them what they need. Be a father to the fatherless, a defender of the weak. Babies can’t pay you back, much like many others. We do this because we love god. We love because he loves us, and it is our role to do so. Be ambitious about the right things. Reality does not bend to our beliefs. If we run into a wall, it hurts. Reality is what hits us when we are wrong. When we are ambitious about the wrong things, reality hits us hard. If you feel meaningless, it might be because you are investing your time in meaningless things.  Meaningless = things that don’t add value to anyone else's life. People rarely applaud the right things. Women like it, but you won't get praised. Everything we attend to is life-giving or ennui-giving.  People who are actively serving others don't struggle with meaninglessness. Invest in meaningless things and you will feel anger toward yourself, yielding irritation toward others, manifesting in odd ways. Am I making her feel more secure? Do my actions indicate that i’m willing to be here for her and the kids? Does she sense that i’m engaged with real life, willing to do what needs to be done for her. Women think risky guys are attractive until they think they are stupid. Men with ambition and not a lot of money are more attractive than lazy men with lots of money. Risk-taking becomes less attractive when she decides to trust you with her future. The initial risk-taking is attractive for your potential. She expects reliability once there is commitment, and for you to become that potential you, not keep swimming in dumb risks. Even dumb jobs are helping people. Work is a noble thing. See the meaning in your job, the bigger purpose and ways you are helping people. Bringing your all to what you are doing is a form of worship, serving God with all your might by serving others well. There is no mess-free work, or relationship. Men are called to be uncomfortable, to bring chaos into order, even though we favor comfort. Relationships require bravery. Rise to the occasion and take on the risk of relationships. Very few men feel like they are good at relationships.  If you can get your mind off yourself, you can make a difference in others’ lives, even if you feel dumb sometimes. No one else is in your exact situation. Wow that’s nice, amazing, attractive, and I don't need to have it. Play the game of contentment, to not covet. I have everything I need to do what I need today. I lack nothing I need. Allow yourself to lose. Ambition is not winning. Be ambitious about losing. It's OK to lose. Athletes are less despaired at loss than their fans. They care about being healthy, the climb, growth. The best ambition is becoming, and assurance in God. We need men who commit, even in a time of options.  We are used to being consumers, looking for upgrades. Commitment means closing doors in favor of a better one, and this is necessary in relationships. Jesus saw women as equals, as belonging. Jesus actively listened to women. It matters to the woman whether something matters to me if it matters to her. Jesus removes shame, rather than exposing and ridiculing it. Our culture treats women as consumables, loving ourselves for the feelings women give us, rather than loving the other human. We treat love as a symptom of our pleasure, not an altruistic choice. Don't live with a woman unless she is your wife. Sex outside of marriage is disintegrity, writing checks with your body that your soul can't keep. Protect them from you (be gentle). Use your words with care. We bless or curse people in every interaction, by what we do and say or communicate to them. The tongue is a sword. Build others up with your words. Kill your sinner self. On your deathbed, you will want relationships, not toys. When you have a family, tending the family garden is your priority. Guard, cultivate, and protect your kids while they are at home. Don't prioritize career and toys over them, don’t show them the wrong example. You can't do everything at once. For now, be the dad they need. Most scares about family life are wrong, except for the heartbreak when your kids move on and grow out of the home. What we pay attention to is everything. We don't struggle with lust, etc., when we are busy with other things. What you pay attention to affects others. A companion is anything you spend your time with. Hang out with fools, and you become more like a fool. Fools love to vent anger, don’t learn from mistakes, hate knowledge of God, can’t handle discipline, argue a lot, quick tempered, mock those who take repentance seriously, too busy telling you their opinions they don't want wisdom. You are very influenced by the company you keep, your companions, what has your attention. Ask God for wisdom. Wisdom is precious, because life is decisions, and reality hurts when we are wrong. Make the wise your companions. What kind of old man do you want to be? Every man over 40 is responsible for his own face. No one wants to experience hell. We are all becoming something, and need to course-correct continuously. You're not getting any younger, and we need you to be an other-centered man. Start now. Honorable men serve as foundations for their family, and may never be fully appreciated behind the grandeur of the building. Stop being needy by giving up traditional models of happiness. Pursue God, and gain persistent joy. Put your hope in God. Your need for admiration costs you admiration. We need men without any significant payoff in mind, who don't need approval. Do you know who you are as a son of God? No man steps in the same river twice. God changes people when they are humble, so ask God to help you be less needy.  If God be for us, who can be against us. Live without fear. God overcomes. Consider the lilies. Don't follow your heart, open your eyes.  Our natural desires and inclinations lead us astray. Take responsibility for your own spiritual life.  God wants your loyalty. Anger is not attractive. Don't be angry, take action. Anger is not a fruit of the spirit. Spirituality is not emotion. Jesus doesn’t talk about emotion. God is pleased by our obedience, not our feeling. If you're not emotional about your faith, don't worry about it. Turn your attention to God. Spirituality is not measured in goosebumps, but in action. It's possible you already know enough to be who you need to be. Put what Jesus said to do into practice. Live as he did. We sometimes prefer complexity and academics because we fear the action. “As long as I don't hurt anyone else” is a lie, pretending we are independent of others. Everything we do has consequences, ripples out. If you're not who you need to be, it hurts you, and hurts us. Do things when you don't feel like it. Have self control.  To gain more self control, change your circumstances and the people around you. Boldly ask God. We are Coworkers in the kingdom of god.
