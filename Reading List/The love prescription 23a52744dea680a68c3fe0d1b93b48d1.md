Notes: You can avoid sweating the small stuff for some things, but for love, it is all about the small stuff. Love is something you do, and action. Make contact, turn toward bids for connection. Avoid the four horsemen: criticism, contempt, defensiveness, stonewalling. Ask a big question. Say thank you. Give a real compliment. Ask for what you need. Reach out and touch, nonsexually. Declare a date night. People change everyday, we should rediscover who they are throughout our relationship. We will learn things we didn’t know, and things that have changed for them.
