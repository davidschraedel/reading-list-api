Notes: I found it funny that he made a mental note to burn his pants when he got home. Funny, clever, and playfully suspenseful. The story is also a story of the character telling his own story, in a way. I liked that the book ended with “he wondered where he would begin”, it makes you want to read it again, because it is his story. It also ends right where he is in the story, anticipating what’s next but not being able to predict it, walking straight into his anxiety and owning whatever happens.
