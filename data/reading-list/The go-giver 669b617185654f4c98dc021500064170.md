Notes: The world is not a dog eat dog place. Test these principles by applying them in your life. People treat you largely as you expect them to treat you. Don’t waste your time. Law of value: give away more value than you are paid. Profitable things will happen. Your true work is determined by how much more you give in value than receive in payment. Give because you love to. This determines your value, your potential. The law of compensation determines how much you get paid. Law of compensation: your income is determined by how many people you serve, and how well you serve them. Your compensation is proportional to how many lives you touch. Anyone can succeed because anyone can give.  There are no limitations on what you can earn, because you can always serve more people. What you focus on is what you get. You can be good and wealthy. Sometimes you feel foolish, look foolish, but you do the thing anyway. Survive, save, and serve: three motives to earn money. Service is the most fulfilling. Do it like you love it. your network is the people who are invested in your success. The law of influence: watch out for the other guy. Your influence is determined by how abundantly you place other people’s interests first. No win-win (tit for  tat), or you owe me attitude. Focus on helping the other guy win. Give. What makes people attractive? They give. Givers are attractive. “You owe me” is you being a creditor, not a friend. Stop keeping score. Care more about their happiness than your own (this is success). The law of authenticity: you are your most valuable commodity. The most valuable gift you have to offer is yourself. 90% people skills. As long as you are trying to be someone else, you have no chance. Be a person (this is people skills, authenticity). The law of receptivity: it’s not better to give than receive, it’s insane to not receive. Every giving can happen only because it is also a receiving. Choose to receive. Don’t refuse the gifts of others. Be open to receive, welcoming. It is more “blessed” to give, but not more important or better. Plenty of people are nice, but don’t succeed. By giving, it is meant being a giving person, giving value to others. Add value to others’ lives. Value comes first, the money you receive comes as a natural result.  Money is not a measure of your goodness, but of your impact.
