Notes: People see progress as always being positive, beneficial, good. In reality, we make choices that have tradeoffs, choosing pros and cons, accepting negatives with positives. Childcare: a woman wants to be a mother to her child, which means that the childcare worker wants to be the mother of her own, not others’ children, and the mother wants to control the childcare, and wants to simultaneously be away for work requirements and be present as a mother with child. This mother-child bond becomes a stubborn zero-sum game between being a high-flying professional and a present caregiver. In the face of this, modern feminists decide that the maternal institution must be a fiction created by the “patriarchy” to serve sexist agendas. And the next goal thereafter is to abolish the idea that heterosexual reproduction and maternal bond are biologically based, and assert they are social constructs. Also to deny that birthing, gestation, and nursing are necessarily done by women.  (Interesting that some arguments levy against the bourgeois,  making the middle class the enemy, with the elites and the lowercase then seen as victims). Childcare services likely create or help cause the “safetyism” of the rising generation, who more than any generation grew up in institutional care settings where safety, the individual, and the interest and reputation of the business were all first-priority. Women have difficulty being mothers since they often need to work, and not every skill as a mother is implicitly learned. Some skills are learned by watching or practicing, and society has offloaded childcare to institutions that won’t demonstrate that care. Thus women fall further behind as mothers as they let others “care” for their children, work more, and increasingly become more ignorant as to the  nature of childrearing.
