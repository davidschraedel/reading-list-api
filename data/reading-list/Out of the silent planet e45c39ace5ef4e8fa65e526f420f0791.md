# Out of the silent planet

Author: C. S. Lewis
Date Finished: May 23, 2024
Finished: Yes

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Notes: Interesting planet that the character explores. Lots of beautiful imagery that changes as you read along, and interesting theological ideas. For example, some beings travel fast enough that they are in more than one place at a time, so would it not be possible that a being could travel fast enough or be of the speed fast enough that the are everywhere at once? Those with evil or moral issues are called bent. Bent meaning that something in them has been distorted or twisted, virtue bent into vice. “Bent one”. They learn Bentness from the exiled God (devil). I liked the description of pink or purple weed, the softness and lesser gravity on the planet, the interaction between species.
