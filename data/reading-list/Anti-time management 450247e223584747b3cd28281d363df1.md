# Anti-time management

Author: Richie norton
Finished: No
Image: Anti-time%20management/Screenshot_20240518-124358_Spotify.jpg
Notes: Life is like a cake. We don’t wait til later to find the sweetness. We put the sugar in at the start and then bake it. We must start living our values now if we want them to be in the end product. Cutting time now to get more later doesn’t work. Time = today is my everything. Prioritize attention. It’s irrational to think that a cake baked without sugar will come out of the oven with sugar in it. We don’t work for money, we work for what we can do with the money. What’s the why of your why? Why do you earn money? Don’t save your dreams, dreams melt like ice cream. The tree is already in the seed. Start with the tree. Making is greater than chasing. The final cause is the seed. Personal purpose: just for you. Professional purpose: for your career, what value to create. People purpose: what people come to mind? What do I need to do for them? People priorities are the most important and difficult to maintain. Play purpose: priorities that fill your soul and energize you. If you could do just one priority from each of these 4 purposes, what would they be? Do those 4 priorities, your priorities of final cause. Focus on these. Put them at the center of your life. Do this as many times as you want. Begin where you are.
